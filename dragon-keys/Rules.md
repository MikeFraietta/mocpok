# MocPok: Dragon Keys

**Two explorers. Two keys. One dragon.**  
A cooperative maze game for two players.

## Your mission

Find both keys and get both explorers through the castle exit before the dragon wakes. You win and lose as a team.

## In the box

- One maze board
- Two explorer pawns
- Two key tokens
- One castle/exit badge
- One dragon marker and its numbered track
- One ordinary six-sided die

## Set up

1. Put the explorers on the two start symbols at the bottom of the maze.
2. Put a key on each key symbol.
3. Put the dragon on **0 for Easy**, **2 for Family**, or **3 for Hard**. The dragon wakes on **12**. Your team gets 12, 10, or 9 rounds respectively.
4. Decide together which explorer will try for each key. Either explorer can collect either or both keys.

## Play a round

1. **Roll once for the team.**
2. **Move both explorers.** Each explorer may take up to the number of steps shown on the die. Choose who moves first.
3. **Collect keys and escape.** Pick up a key as soon as an explorer reaches its space. Both keys belong to the whole team.
4. **Move the dragon one space** after both explorers have had their movement, unless you have already won.

## Movement

- One step moves to the next square through an open passage.
- Move up, down, left, or right. Never move diagonally or through a wall.
- You may turn around, stop early, or wait. Unused steps do not carry over.
- Explorers may pass each other or share a space. Briefly lift one pawn if needed.

## The castle exit

Put the castle badge on EXIT during setup. Move the badge aside as soon as the team has both keys: the exit is now open. An explorer reaching it then escapes: put that pawn beside the board. If an explorer is already waiting at the gate when the second key is collected, it escapes immediately.

**Both explorers escape: you win immediately.** You can win during your last round, before the dragon moves.

**The dragon reaches 12 while someone is still inside: try again together.**

## Team tips

Plan your routes before rolling. Split up to collect the keys. Point out open passages and help each other count steps.

## Grown-up setup note

This prototype uses a shared die roll each round, but each explorer receives the full number rolled. Do not split one roll between the explorers. The finish is fixed at 12; changing the dragon's starting position sets the difficulty.
